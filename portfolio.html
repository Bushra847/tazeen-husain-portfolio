Teaching Philosophy

"My teaching philosophy is grounded in the belief that all students deserve learning experiences that reflect who they are, challenge their thinking, and support their growth."

The Process

I view teaching as a relational and collaborative process, where students' identities, cultures, and lived experiences shape the learning environment.

Creating classrooms that are equitable, inclusive, and student-centered.

Methodology

Prioritizing differentiated instruction and multimodal learning to reach every unique learner.

I use visual supports, hands-on materials, and technology to meet students where they are. At the heart of my work is a commitment to Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion (EDI)—aiming to remove barriers, amplify student voice, and model empathy in every interaction.
